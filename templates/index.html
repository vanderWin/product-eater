This tool cleans and standardises product data from exported feeds. Upload a TSV or CSV, preview your columns, normalise key attributes, and download a refined dataset.

Instructions

Getting started

  • Log into Merchant Center, navigate to the right client, open the Products tab, and export all products.
  • Unpack the archive Google gives you so you can access the TSV.
  • Browse for your TSV or drop it into the upload area below, then move through the sections.

Section 1: Preview and select columns — Pick the columns you need for analysis.

Section 2: Filters — Filter to your market, availability, condition, etc.

Section 3: Product grouping — Group SKUs into product groups via item_group_id or image URL.

Section 4: Colour summary — Review colour distribution.

Section 5: Colour mapping — Standardise brand colour names to generic colours.

Section 6: Category extraction — Split product_type into first / penultimate / final segments.

Section 7: Normalised feed download — Export the cleaned feed.

Section 8: Keyword combinations — Combine fields to create keyword lists.

Section 9: Combined keyword list — Review and export combined keywords.

Section 10: Google Ads search volumes — Fetch monthly search volumes via the API.

Section 11: Keywords + Google Ads metrics — Opportunity scoring and exports.
